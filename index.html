Historical Precedent — Why Triple Trigger Matters
2012–13 UPA-II: Diesel at ₹52, LPG at ₹410, food inflation at 11.5% — all three converging simultaneously. Voter anger crystallised into the 2014 BJP landslide (UPA reduced to 44 seats from 206).  ·  2021–22: Petrol crossed ₹110 in Oct 2021. Centre cut excise by ₹5 before UP elections. Bought 18 months of headroom.  ·  May 2026: Four hikes in 11 days (₹7.50/L cumulative) after 76-day freeze. OMCs absorbed ~₹1,000 Cr/day losses. Hormuz disruption + rupee at ₹96 = both crude and FX working against India simultaneously.  ·  Pattern: Each individual trigger is manageable. All three simultaneously = the cushion breaks — the government runs out of single levers large enough to compensate, and media externalisation ("global phenomenon") stops working because household pain is visible across all three daily-use categories at once.  ·  The upstream signal: BDTI spikes, Urals spread narrows, FII outflows, rupee weakens — these move 4–8 weeks before the pump price. The Early Warning Radar catches the pressure before it reaches households.
